Why it's fair

Three things that make Tombola different from a centralized raffle.

01

Verifiable randomness

Switchboard On-Demand posts a signed reveal on-chain. No team can predict or influence the result before the draw.

a3 7f 22 e1 9c 5b 04 d7 3a 88 f2 11 be 06 a5 cf

Random bytes from Switchboard, parsed via audited byte-offsets (D-035).

02

Instant payouts

When the winner is picked, the entire pot lands in their wallet in the same transaction. No claims, no delay.

From SOL to win in three steps.

01

Pick a pool, buy a ticket

Each ticket is 0.01 SOL. The more you hold, the higher your chance.

02

Wait for the draw

When the round closes, Switchboard On-Demand posts a verifiable random number on-chain.

03

Auto-payout to winner

Winning ticket gets the pot (minus 0.5% fee) directly to their wallet. No claim step.
