5 · Ocean vs Cosmic — quick read

OCEAN · Pros

  • Concrete creatures — easy to recognize at any size, "I'm a hammerhead" reads in a second
  • Strong cultural hook (whales = high-value users is already DeFi vocabulary)
  • Rich naming inventory — 100 distinct sea creatures already exist
  • Each badge can be illustrative + memorable

OCEAN · Cons

  • Feels "warmer" / kids-game-adjacent — may not match a serious raffle product
  • Mid-tier creatures (e.g. sardine vs anchovy) blur together
  • Less obvious endgame escalation than cosmic

COSMIC · Pros

  • Premium, futuristic, on-brand for Web3 / on-chain product
  • Strong endgame potential — black hole / nebula / cosmic god reads as elite
  • Abstract shapes scale well to tiny badge sizes
  • Animated tier-6 rim feels rare without being childish

COSMIC · Cons

  • Mid-tier naming is harder (how many distinct stars / planets / galaxies can you name?)
  • Less personal — "I'm a planet" feels less identifiable than "I'm a shark"
  • Risk of feeling generic — every Web3 product uses cosmic motifs

My honest take

Both work. Cosmic is the safer, more premium choice — it matches Tombola's existing dark/cyan/lavender brand language and the "trustless · on-chain" technical vibe. Ocean is the more characterful, identity-driven choice — but it's also a bigger creative bet, and shares vocabulary with thousands of other DeFi products that already use "whale" / "shrimp" as informal status.

If I had to ship one tomorrow: cosmic. If I had two weeks to commission custom illustrations and the brand wants more warmth: ocean.

Reply with "go ocean", "go cosmic", or "keep both as user-selectable themes" — that last option is also feasible (one extra setting toggle), but cost is roughly 2× the badge work.
