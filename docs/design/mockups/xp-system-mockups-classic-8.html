3 · 8-tier vs original 6-tier · vs ocean / cosmic

8-CLASSIC · pros

  • Tier names are universally understood in crypto — shrimp, dolphin, whale carry instant social meaning
  • One creature per tier = each one gets a bespoke illustration · easier to make iconic
  • Apex tier "Hump Back" is recognizable as the largest whale variant, but distinct from "whale" (the tier just below)
  • Maps cleanly onto common crypto status displays — fits the audience's mental model
  • 8 tiers is the most common count in crypto status pages (Binance VIPs, Bybit VIPs, etc.)

8-CLASSIC · cons

  • Less original — every DeFi product uses this exact taxonomy
  • Only one creature per tier · no internal escalation feeling within a tier (e.g. "I'm a Shark Lv. 70" feels weaker than "I'm a Hammerhead → Mako progression")
  • "Hump Back" as the apex is technically a whale variant — less mythic than "Poseidon's Crown"

6-TIER OCEAN · pros

  • Each tier holds 15–25 sub-creatures · constant little level-up "you're now a Mako shark"
  • Mythic tier escalates beyond known creatures into named legendary beasts
  • More personalization — the L72 user has a different identity from L78 even though both are "whales" in the casual sense
  • Strong endgame: T6 mythic creatures get animated holographic treatment

6-TIER OCEAN · cons

  • 100 distinct creature names is a lot of art work — even procedural, each needs a custom SVG
  • Mid-tier names blur (sardine vs anchovy · sei whale vs minke whale)
  • Less universally legible — "I'm a Pilot Whale" needs more explanation than "I'm a Shark"

My honest take

The 8-classic system is the easier ship and the safer bet. It speaks crypto's native vocabulary, requires only 8 bespoke badges (not 100), and the apex Hump Back with the gold holographic rim is still rare and aspirational. Sub-levels can still happen — a "Lv. 47 Fish" or "Lv. 63 Shark" still reads great — but the badge identity locks at the tier level. That's a feature, not a bug: users brag about their tier, not their sub-level.

The 6-tier ocean is the more elaborate product. Worth doing if you want users to feel constant micro-progression and if you're willing to fund 100 custom badge illustrations. The mythic tier is genuinely beautiful at L100 in a way the Hump Back can't quite match.

If you said "ship the simplest version tomorrow": 8-classic. If you said "make this our defining product feature": 6-tier ocean with the mythic endgame.
