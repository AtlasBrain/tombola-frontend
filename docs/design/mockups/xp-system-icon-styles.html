Quick read

A · Cartoon is the easiest to read at a glance — every creature is unmistakable. Closest to your reference image. Best for landing pages + onboarding hype.

B · Flat geometric is the most "design-system native" — clean, no gradients, ages well. Best for dashboards.

C · Line art is the most "premium technical product" feel. Strong on profile pages, weak in tiny table cells.

D · Pixel art leans hardest into the "collectible game" framing. Most distinctive of the five. Risk: feels too gamey if Tombola wants to look serious.

E · Silhouette wins on the smallest surfaces (table rows, friend list, mention pills). Loses character at large sizes.

My pick: A · Cartoon for the badge surface (profile page, level-up moments) + E · Silhouette for inline contexts (table rows, pseudo mentions). Two variants per creature instead of one. Roughly 2× the badge work but each variant is small.
