IdentityPill — design variants

Hover each pill to see the on-brand effect (tear-corner clip + caret rotate). Pick one.

1 · Tear-corner white RECOMMENDED — matches "LEARN MORE" button

Same visual language as the hero's LEARN MORE CTA: white background, ticket-corner clip-path that opens up on hover, lavender chip caret that rotates 45° on hover. Pure brand consistency, zero new visual vocabulary.

PROS

Re-uses the existing fx-tear CSS — zero new effects to maintain. Reads as a sibling of LEARN MORE / BUY A TICKET. The 45° caret rotate on hover is your signature flourish.

CONS

White surface still slightly louder than the rest of the right cluster (bell + search are dark). Mitigated by matching the LEARN MORE button which is also white.
2 · Tear-corner mint on-brand · primary accent

Like variant 1 but the tear surface is MINT — matches the BUY A TICKET button's lavender cousin in spirit (color-coded CTA). Black caret circle instead of lavender so it doesn't fight the mint.

PROS

Strong identity — your wallet pill becomes a brand-color anchor on every page. Mint reads as "you" (private pools use mint everywhere).

CONS

Loud. Two CTAs already (LEARN MORE + BUY A TICKET); a third mint surface in the header can pull attention away from the hero.
3 · Dark outline pill quietest · matches SEARCH chip

Same chrome as the SEARCH button: 1px neutral-800 border, transparent fill, mono label. The whole right cluster (search, bell, identity) now reads as one family of dark chips. Caret is a small grey circle.

PROS

Cluster cohesion — the entire right side reads as one design family. Doesn't shout. Lets the hero CTAs do the loud work.

CONS

Identity becomes visually equal to "search" — for a wallet-gated product some operators want the wallet to feel more important. Loses the hover delight from variants 1/2.
4 · Dark + mint pulse subtle accent · "you're connected" indicator

Mostly the same dark chrome as variant 3 (so it lives in the same chip family as SEARCH and BELL), but with a small mint pulsing dot in front of the pseudo — same dot you use on "LIVE · 4 POOLS RUNNING". Reads as "you are online".

PROS

Quiet but alive — the pulsing dot signals "wallet active" without taking real-estate. Visual sibling of the LIVE pill, so already in your design vocabulary.

CONS

Animated dots add a tiny visual draw that competes with the LIVE pill on the landing page. Consider only animating on the homepage / disabling elsewhere.
5 · Mint outline pill middle ground

Mint outline + tinted background — the same recipe as your OPEN status pill but applied to identity. Mint reads as "you" without being a full mint fill. Caret becomes mint-on-mint until hover where it fills.

PROS

On-brand mint without shouting. Caret fill on hover gives the same affordance as variants 1/2.

CONS

Visually similar to the OPEN status pill — could create momentary confusion (is my wallet "open"?). Less of a problem when the avatar is present.
6 · Lavender caret on dark minimal change · just swap the white pill for dark

Closest to your current implementation — same lavender caret circle (with the 45° rotate hover), but on a dark border-neutral-800 chrome instead of a white pill. One-line CSS swap from the live version.

PROS

Least disruptive change — keeps the lavender caret you've been using. Just darkens the pill chrome so it fits the rest of the right cluster.

CONS

Lavender on dark border is a colorway you haven't really used elsewhere — net-new visual signal.
My pick PICK ONE — TELL ME THE NUMBER

Variant 1 (tear-corner white) gives you the most brand consistency — it pairs the IdentityPill with LEARN MORE / BUY A TICKET and re-uses the existing fx-tear CSS. The lavender caret-rotate on hover is signature delight.

Variant 4 (dark + pulse) is the second pick if you'd rather have the entire right cluster (search + bell + identity) read as one quiet chip family, with just a tiny mint heartbeat to indicate "you are connected".

Everything else is reasonable — but 1 and 4 are the cleanest fits.
